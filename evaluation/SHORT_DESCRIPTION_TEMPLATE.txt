Ten candidate tasks test idempotent payment capture, refund ceilings, atomic double-entry posting, out-of-order events, settlement reconciliation, precise monetary arithmetic, and durable inbox/outbox handling. Each task has a behavioral grader, a known-correct reference and deliberately wrong controls.
